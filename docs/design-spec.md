# Homepage design reference checklist

Reference: user-provided 941 × 1672 long screenshot. The production target is a responsive component implementation, not a rasterized page.

1. Pale blue utility strip with contact/language controls.
2. White navigation with MAIGE brand, six primary destinations, search and green quote button.
3. Light blue hero with large navy headline, product search, popular terms, two CTAs, architecture photo and four benefit badges.
4. Four category cards.
5. Four featured cards.
6. Pale advantage strip with four icon/text items.
7. Four facility cards.
8. Four application cards.
9. Four resource cards.
10. Light contact strip with details and enquiry form.
11. Dark navy footer with five columns and legal links.

Implemented desktop-first at 1440px, with layout rules for 1920px, 1024px, 768px, 390px and 375px. Arabic uses `dir=rtl` and localized labels, while URLs, product terms and email values retain readable direction. Screenshot imagery remains temporary until original photographs arrive.
